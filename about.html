She built a reputation by doing things properly

Zlata has 15 years of cleaning experience and has been running her own business for 10. She built her reputation the old-fashioned way: turning up on time, getting on with it, and leaving every job the way she would want her own home left. 65 five-star reviews on Google. No paid ads, no agency, just word of mouth.

She works alone. That is not a limitation, it is the point. When you book Zlata, you know exactly who is coming through your door. Same face. Same standard. Every single visit. No strangers. No surprises.

She covers all kinds of cleans, from a regular weekly tidy-up to full post-renovation deep cleans, office cleaning, Airbnb turnarounds and end-of-tenancy clears. She also does chimney sweeps and floor polishing, so she handles things that most cleaners will not.

Zlata is based in Kilkenny and works across the city and county. All quotes are per job, given by phone or WhatsApp. Ring or message, describe what you need, and she will give you a straight price.
