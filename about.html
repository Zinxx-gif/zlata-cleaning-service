She made a business out of doing things properly

Zlata has been cleaning homes and businesses in Kilkenny for years. She built her reputation the old-fashioned way: by turning up on time, getting on with it, and leaving every job the way she would want her own home left.

She works alone. That is not a limitation; it is the point. When you book Zlata, you know exactly who is coming through your door. Same face, same standard, every single visit. No strangers. No surprises.

She covers all kinds of cleans: from a regular weekly tidy-up to full post-renovation deep cleans and everything in between. She also does chimney sweeps and floor polishing, so she can handle things that standard cleaners will not touch.

Zlata is based in Kilkenny and works across the city and county. All bookings are taken by phone. Ring, describe what you need, and she will give you a straight quote.
