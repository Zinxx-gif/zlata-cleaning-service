Callan cleaned the right way

Callan is a market town about 16km west of Kilkenny city, sitting between the Kings River and the older streets that stretch back past St. Mary's Church. The Augustinian friary ruins are a reminder that this place has been here a long time. The houses around it deserve the same level of care.

Zlata covers Callan as part of her regular schedule out from Kilkenny. With 15 years of experience and 10 years running her own business, she knows how to assess a house, quote it fairly, and deliver a clean that makes an actual difference.

She charges per job, not per hour. No guessing how long it will take and what the final bill will be. Ring or WhatsApp, describe what you need, and she will give you a clear price.

She covers Callan town and the surrounding areas including Knocktopher, Stoneyford, Kells and the parishes in between.
