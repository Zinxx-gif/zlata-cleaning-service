Cleaning Service Kilkenny

Zlata is Kilkenny. This is her home patch. She has been cleaning homes here for 15 years and knows the city inside out.

Kilkenny homes looked after properly

From the Marble City streets to the residential estates out past Freshford Road and Hebron, Zlata cleans homes all across Kilkenny. She has been doing it for 15 years, 10 of those running her own business under her own name. She is not a franchise and she is not an agency. She is a local.

Kilkenny is a city that takes pride in its appearance. The castle draws visitors from all over the world, the medieval streetscape is immaculate, and most residents hold their homes to the same standard. That suits Zlata fine. She holds herself to a high standard too.

She covers Kilkenny city centre, Freshford Road, Newpark, Loughboy, Callan Road, Hebron, Dunmore, Ennisnag, and all the surrounding townlands. If you're within the city or county, ring and she will let you know if she covers your area.

All quotes are given per job, not per hour. Ring or WhatsApp to describe what you need and she will give you a straight price.
