Window Cleaning Service Kilkenny

Clean windows make the whole house feel different. Zlata does them inside and out, no streaks.

Let the light back in

Dirty windows are one of those things that creep up on you. You stop noticing them until one day you do, and then you can't stop. Clean windows make a room feel bigger, brighter and better maintained.

Zlata cleans windows inside and out across Kilkenny and the surrounding area. She uses the right products for a streak-free result and wipes down frames at the same time. Not the kind of job that needs explaining for half an hour.

Window cleaning is often added on to a standard clean or a deep clean. Let Zlata know when you ring and she will factor it into the quote.

What is included

  • Interior window clean on all accessible windows
  • Exterior window clean on ground and first floor
  • Window frame wipe-down inside and out
  • Sill cleaned
  • Streak-free finish on all panes

Access and height

Exterior window cleaning is carried out from ground level or from a stable ladder for first-floor windows. Upper-floor windows on taller properties may require specialist equipment not included in this service. Mention any particularly high windows when you ring so expectations are clear.

Get a quote

Ring Zlata. She will give you a price based on the number of windows and access.
