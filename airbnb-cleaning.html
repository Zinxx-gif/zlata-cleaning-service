Airbnb Cleaning Service Kilkenny

Guest checking out at 11, next guest arriving at 3. Zlata gets the property turned around properly so your reviews stay strong.

Five-star clean every turnaround

Kilkenny is a popular destination year-round, from the festival weekends to the hurling fixtures to the general tourist flow through the Marble City. If you are running an Airbnb or short-term let here, your listing's reputation depends on what guests find when they walk in.

Zlata has 15 years of cleaning experience and understands what a proper turnaround involves. It is not just a quick hoover and a wipe of the surfaces. Beds stripped and remade, bathroom cleaned and restocked, kitchen reset, floors done, anything the previous guests left behind dealt with. The property ready for the next guest, not just presentable.

All turnaround cleans are quoted per property. Ring or WhatsApp to discuss your schedule, the size of the property and any specific requirements. Zlata is available seven days a week, 9am to 9pm, which covers most checkout and check-in windows.

What is included

  • All beds stripped, remade with fresh linen (linen provided by host)
  • Bathroom cleaned, toilet, sink and shower scrubbed
  • Kitchen wiped down, dishes checked and any left items dealt with
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Surfaces dusted and wiped
  • Bins emptied and new liners put in
  • Toiletries and supplies restocked if provided
  • Property check-over before leaving

Reliable on a schedule

If you have a regular booking calendar, Zlata can work around it. WhatsApp her the checkout dates as they come in and she will schedule around your availability. Consistency matters when you are trying to maintain a rating.
