Bennettsbridge, just down the road

Bennettsbridge is a village about 7km south of Kilkenny city on the River Nore. Known for the Cushendale Woollen Mills and a strong tradition of crafts and pottery, it is a quiet, characterful place with a mix of older cottages and newer homes. It is also one of the closest villages to Kilkenny city, which means Zlata can get there quickly and easily.

She has been cleaning homes in Bennettsbridge and the surrounding area for years. The short distance from her base in Kilkenny means she can be flexible on scheduling and responsive on short notice.

Every job is quoted per property, not per hour. Whether it is a one-bedroom cottage or a four-bedroom family home, she assesses it and gives you a fixed price before she starts. Ring or WhatsApp to get the conversation going.
