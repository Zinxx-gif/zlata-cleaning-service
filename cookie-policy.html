Cookie Policy

We keep it simple. This site uses no advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics.

Last updated: June 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. Similar technologies such as localStorage serve a comparable purpose and are also covered by this policy.

What we use

This site uses a single item of localStorage to remember your cookie consent choice. No value is recorded until you click Accept or Decline on the consent banner.

Consent preference (localStorage)

Name Purpose Duration
cookieConsent Stores whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner Until you clear browser data
_vercel_analytics Vercel Analytics: counts page views and basic engagement. No personal data is recorded. IP addresses are anonymised by Vercel before storage. Session

What we do not use

  • ✓ No Google Analytics or any third-party analytics
  • ✓ No advertising or remarketing pixels
  • ✓ No social media tracking cookies
  • ✓ No cookies that follow you across other websites

Infrastructure

This site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may set short-lived technical cookies for routing and performance purposes as part of their infrastructure. These are strictly necessary for the site to function and are not used for tracking. See Vercel's privacy policy for details.

How to manage cookies

You can clear your consent preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or site data. Instructions vary by browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
