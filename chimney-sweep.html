Chimney Sweep Service Kilkenny

A blocked chimney is a fire risk. Zlata sweeps it properly, leaves no mess, and gets out of your way.

A clean chimney draws better

Most people only think about their chimney when something goes wrong. Don't wait for a slow draw, a smell, or worse. An annual sweep keeps the flue clear, improves the draw on your fire, and significantly reduces the risk of a chimney fire.

Zlata provides chimney sweep services in Kilkenny and the surrounding area. She works neatly, takes care of the fireplace area, and leaves no soot on your carpet or furniture. The job takes under an hour in most cases.

The best time to book is before the burning season starts. Don't leave it until October when every chimney sweep in the county is booked out.

What is included

  • Full flue sweep from firebox to pot
  • Soot and creosote removed
  • Ash cleared from firebox
  • Fireplace surround and hearth cleaned
  • Dust sheets laid to protect flooring
  • All debris bagged and removed
  • Visual check for obvious blockages

Important to know

Zlata sweeps solid fuel fires, open fires and stove flues. If you have a gas appliance, the flue should be serviced by a registered gas installer. Not sure which you have? Ring and describe it and she will advise.

If your chimney has not been swept in several years, there may be significant creosote buildup. Mention this when you ring so the right amount of time can be allocated.
