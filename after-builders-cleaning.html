After Builders Cleaning Kilkenny

Construction dust finds its way into everything. Zlata gets it back out again. Every trace.

Getting your home back

Renovation work leaves a mess that takes far longer to clean than most people expect. Fine plaster dust settles on every surface, inside every cupboard, on every sill. Paint flecks on tiles, silicone smears on glass, grout dust on floors. It is not a job for a standard clean.

Zlata handles post-renovation and post-construction cleans across Kilkenny and the surrounding area. She also does end-of-tenancy cleans, move-in cleans, and handovers where the property needs a complete reset.

Every job is quoted individually. The time needed depends on the scale of the renovation and the size of the property. Ring and describe the job and Zlata will give you a straight quote.

What is included

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces
  • Plaster and paint splatter cleaned from tiles, glass and woodwork
  • All floors swept, mopped and polished
  • Windows and frames cleaned inside
  • Kitchen cupboards and surfaces wiped inside and out
  • Bathroom fully scrubbed and de-scaled
  • All fixtures and fittings wiped down
  • Silicone and grout cleaned
  • Debris cleared and bins emptied

Moving in or out?

Moving cleans and end-of-tenancy cleans follow the same thorough process. Whether you need the property spotless for new tenants or want to settle into a genuinely clean home, Zlata will sort it out. For properties with newly installed floors, she also offers floor polishing and buffering as part of the same visit.
