Transparent pricing

What affects the price

Every quote is given by phone or WhatsApp before any work starts. No surprises, no hidden fees.

🏠

Size of the home

A 3-bed house takes longer than a 1-bed apartment. Most standard cleans in a 3-bed home run 2–3 hours. Bigger homes with more rooms and surfaces take more time and cost more accordingly.

🛁

Number of bathrooms

Bathrooms are one of the most time-intensive parts of any clean. A home with three bathrooms will take noticeably longer than one with one. Zlata factors this in when quoting.

✨

Condition of the property

A home that hasn't had a professional clean in over a year needs more work than one cleaned monthly. That extra effort is reflected in the price, honestly and upfront.

📋

Type of clean

Standard cleans cost less than deep cleans or after-builders work. Post-build jobs involve construction dust, grout residue and debris at a different level of intensity. The price reflects that.

💬 Every quote is given by phone or WhatsApp before anything is booked. Ring or message with your home size, number of bathrooms, and what you need done. Zlata will give you a clear price. No hourly guessing, no bill shock at the end.

🎥 To give you an accurate price quickly, Zlata will usually ask for a short video walkthrough of the house sent by WhatsApp. A one-minute walk around is enough. It saves a back-and-forth and means the price she gives you is exact, not a rough guess.

Things people ask

How do I get a quote? +

Ring or WhatsApp 085 784 5186. Tell Zlata what you need and where you are. She will usually ask you to send a quick video walkthrough of the house on WhatsApp so she can see the size and condition and give you an accurate price straight away. No site visits needed, no waiting for a callback. Most people have a price within a few minutes of messaging.

Does Zlata charge by the hour? +

No. Every job is quoted per job, not per hour. You agree a flat price before the clean starts. There is no clock running while she works, no reason for her to pad out the time, and no surprise bill when she finishes. What you agree upfront is what you pay.

What areas does Zlata cover? +

Kilkenny city and county, plus surrounding towns within roughly 50km. This includes Thomastown, Callan, Castlecomer, Bennettsbridge, Freshford, Graiguenamanagh, Urlingford, Piltown, and more. If you are outside Kilkenny and unsure whether she covers your area, just ring or message and she will tell you straight away.

Is oven cleaning included? +

No, oven cleaning is not included in a standard or deep clean as standard. It is available as an extra and carries an additional charge. If you want the oven done, just mention it when you ring or message and Zlata will add it to your quote.

Does Zlata clean windows from outside? +

No. Window cleaning covers interior panes only. Exterior windows are not part of the service. If you need exterior windows cleaned, ask when you call and she can advise on options.

Not sure which service you need? Just ring. Zlata will figure it out with you.
