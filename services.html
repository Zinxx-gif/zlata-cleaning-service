What Zlata offers

Services that actually get it clean

No cookie-cutter packages. Ring and describe what you need. Zlata will sort you out with a straight quote.

The full list

🧹

Standard Clean

2-3 hours

The regular catch-up clean. Covers all the key areas that build up week to week. Great for maintaining a tidy home without lifting a finger yourself.

  • Kitchen surfaces and appliance exteriors
  • Bathroom and toilet
  • Bedrooms tidied and surfaces wiped
  • Dusting throughout
  • Mopping hard floors
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
✨

Deep Clean

4-5+ hours

The full works. Everything that the standard clean covers, plus all the places that get overlooked. Ideal for a spring clean, before guests arrive, or when a property has been neglected.

  • Everything in the standard clean
  • Inside ovens and microwaves
  • Inside cupboards and drawers
  • Behind and under appliances
  • Window sills and frames inside
  • Skirting boards, light switches, door handles
  • Lime scale removal in bathrooms
🏗️

After Builders / Renovation / Moving Cleans

Call for time and quote

Construction dust gets everywhere. Paint flecks, plaster, silicone smears. Zlata handles post-renovation and post-construction cleans, as well as end-of-tenancy and move-in cleans where a full reset is needed.

  • Fine dust removal from all surfaces
  • Paint and plaster splatter cleanup
  • Window and frame cleaning
  • Kitchen and bathroom deep scrub
  • Floor polish and mop after debris cleared
✨

Floor Polishing and Buffering

Call for time and quote

Hard floors lose their lustre over time. Professional polishing and buffering restores the finish on timber, tile, vinyl and laminate floors. Makes a real difference to how a room looks and feels.

  • Timber and hardwood floor polish
  • Tile and stone buffering
  • Vinyl and laminate restore
  • Pre-polish clean and prep included
🔥

Chimney Sweep

Call for time and quote

Don't wait until winter to find out your chimney is blocked. A clean chimney draws better and is safer. Zlata provides a thorough sweep and checks for any obvious blockages or buildup.

  • Full flue sweep
  • Soot and ash removal
  • Fireplace area cleaned
  • No mess left behind
🪟

Window Cleaning

Call for time and quote

Streak-free windows inside and out. Simple job that makes an immediate difference to how bright your home looks and feels.

  • Interior window clean
  • Exterior window clean
  • Window frame wipe-down
  • Streak-free finish
