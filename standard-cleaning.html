Standard Cleaning Service Kilkenny

The regular catch-up clean. Everything you need done, done properly, on a schedule that works for you.

What gets done

A standard clean covers all the areas that accumulate mess in the average week. Kitchen surfaces, hob and splashback, sink, bathroom, toilet, all bedrooms tidied, floors mopped and hoovered. It is the clean that keeps your home feeling under control without you having to think about it.

Zlata works alone. That means she takes her time, does it properly, and you don't have a rotation of different faces coming through your door every fortnight. Same person, same standard, every visit.

Most standard cleans take 2 to 3 hours depending on the size of the property. Ring for a quote and she will give you a straight answer on time and price.

What is included

  • Kitchen surfaces, hob, splashback and sink
  • Appliance exteriors (fridge, microwave, dishwasher)
  • Bathroom scrubbed, toilet cleaned
  • All bedrooms tidied, beds made if needed
  • Dusting throughout (shelves, skirting, furniture tops)
  • Hard floors mopped
  • Carpets and rugs vacuumed
  • Bins emptied

Good to know

Standard cleans are great as a once-off or on a regular schedule (weekly, fortnightly, monthly). The more regular the clean, the faster and easier each visit tends to be. Call Zlata to set up a recurring arrangement.

If your home needs a more thorough reset first, a deep clean may be a better starting point.
