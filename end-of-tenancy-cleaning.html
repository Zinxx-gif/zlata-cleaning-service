End of Tenancy Cleaning Kilkenny

Moving out and need the deposit back? Zlata gets the property to inspection standard. Every room, every surface.

Get your deposit back

End of tenancy cleans are not the same as a standard clean. Landlords and letting agents inspect to a high standard, and a half-hearted clean won't pass. Zlata has been doing end-of-tenancy cleans in Kilkenny for 15 years. She knows what gets checked and she makes sure it's done.

The job covers every room in the property, inside every cupboard, oven cleaned, bathroom descaled, carpets vacuumed, windows done. She charges per property, not per hour. You get a clear price before she starts.

Ring or WhatsApp to describe the property, the number of bedrooms and any specific requirements from your landlord or letting agent. Zlata will give you a straight quote and work around your moving schedule.

What is included

  • All rooms cleaned top to bottom
  • Kitchen fully cleaned including oven and inside all cupboards
  • Bathroom and toilet descaled and scrubbed
  • All surfaces wiped including skirting boards and door handles
  • Carpets vacuumed throughout
  • Hard floors mopped
  • Windows cleaned inside
  • Bins emptied and property left ready for inspection

Also covers move-in cleans

Moving into a rental and want it properly clean before you unpack? Zlata can do a move-in clean to the same standard. Ring or WhatsApp and describe the situation and she will sort a quote.
