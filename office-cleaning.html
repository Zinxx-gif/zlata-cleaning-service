💼 Commercial and office cleaning

Office Cleaning Service Kilkenny

A clean office is not a luxury, it's a baseline. Zlata keeps Kilkenny businesses presentable, consistently.

Your business, looking the part

Zlata's commercial cleaning is suited to small and medium premises in Kilkenny: offices, clinics, salons, therapy rooms, retail units, and similar spaces. She is not a contract cleaning company sending different staff every week. She is one person who takes the job seriously and delivers the same standard each visit.

With 15 years of cleaning experience, Zlata knows how to work around a business schedule. Early morning before staff arrive, after closing, or on a day that suits your operation. She is flexible, reliable, and she won't need chasing.

All commercial cleans are quoted per premises. Ring or WhatsApp to describe the space, the frequency you need, and any specific requirements, and Zlata will come back with a clear, per-visit price.

What is included

  • Desks, counters and work surfaces cleaned and sanitised
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Toilets and bathroom facilities cleaned
  • Kitchen or staff area wiped down
  • Bins emptied throughout
  • Glass doors and partitions cleaned
  • Entrance and reception area presented properly
  • Windows cleaned on request

Suited to

Small offices, letting agencies, salons, beauty clinics, physio and therapy practices, dental waiting areas, retail units and similar commercial spaces. For larger industrial or warehouse premises, ring and describe the space and Zlata will advise honestly on whether it is a fit.
