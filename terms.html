Terms and Conditions

Straight terms, plain language. No small print designed to confuse.

Last updated: June 2026 • Zlata's Cleaning Service, Kilkenny, Ireland • Tel: 085 784 5186

1. Who we are

Zlata's Cleaning Service is a sole trader cleaning business based in Kilkenny, Ireland, operated by Zlata personally. When you book a clean, you are booking Zlata directly. There are no subcontractors, no agency staff, and no third parties involved in carrying out the work.

2. Services

Services offered include standard home cleaning, deep cleaning, after-builders and post-renovation cleaning, end-of-tenancy cleaning, Airbnb and short-let turnaround cleaning, office and commercial cleaning, floor polishing, chimney sweeping, window cleaning (interior only), and carpet cleaning. Details of each service are available on the Services page of this website.

Availability is subject to scheduling. Zlata operates Monday to Friday, 9am to 9pm. She will confirm availability when you call or WhatsApp to discuss your job.

3. Quotes and pricing

All quotes are given per job, not per hour. A price is agreed by phone or WhatsApp before any work begins. The quoted price is fixed for the job as described. If the scope changes on the day (for example the property is significantly larger or in a worse condition than described), Zlata will discuss any adjustment before proceeding.

Quotes are free and carry no obligation to book.

4. What is included and excluded

Each service has its own scope. As a general rule:

  • Zlata supplies her own cleaning materials and equipment. You do not need to provide anything.
  • Oven cleaning is not included in standard or deep clean pricing. It can be added as an extra — mention it when you call and it will be factored into your quote.
  • Window cleaning covers interior panes only. Exterior windows are not part of this service.
  • Items that require specialist treatment (antiques, fine art, delicate fabrics) are excluded from standard cleaning. Please mention these when booking.

If you are unsure whether something is included, ask when you ring. It takes ten seconds and avoids any misunderstanding on the day.

5. Payment

Payment is due on completion of the work. Accepted methods include cash and bank transfer. Zlata will confirm payment details when you book.

For recurring arrangements (weekly, fortnightly, or monthly cleans), the same agreed rate applies to each visit unless the scope changes or a new quote is agreed.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give as much notice as possible. A minimum of 24 hours notice is appreciated. This allows Zlata to adjust her schedule and take other bookings.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a short-notice charge, particularly for larger or specialist jobs where significant time has been blocked out. This will be discussed and agreed at the time of booking if it applies.

7. Access to the property

You will need to arrange access to the property at the agreed time. This can be done by being present yourself, leaving a key, or using a key safe. The access method will be agreed when you book.

If access cannot be gained at the agreed time through no fault of Zlata, the visit may be treated as a cancellation with short notice.

8. Liability and damage

Zlata takes care with your property. If something is accidentally damaged during a clean, notify her as soon as possible. Any claim for accidental damage must be reported within 24 hours of the clean being completed.

Zlata is not liable for pre-existing damage, wear that becomes more apparent after cleaning, or damage caused by items that were not mentioned as fragile or valuable before the clean began.

These terms do not affect your statutory rights as a consumer under Irish law.

9. Conduct

Zlata reserves the right to refuse or discontinue a job if she encounters conditions that are unsafe, abusive, or materially different from what was described when booking (for example, a property significantly beyond what was discussed). In such cases, a partial charge may apply for time already spent.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland. Any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Irish courts.
