Floor Polishing and Buffering Kilkenny

Hard floors lose their finish over time. Zlata restores them. Timber, tile, vinyl, laminate.

Floors that look the part again

Floors take the most punishment in any home. Foot traffic, furniture marks, dulling from cleaning products, years of wear. A regular mop keeps them clean, but it doesn't restore their finish. That's what polishing and buffering does.

Zlata offers professional floor polishing and buffering across Kilkenny and surrounding areas. She works with timber, hardwood, tile, stone, vinyl and laminate. The result is a surface that looks properly cared for, not just swept.

The price depends on floor type, condition and area size. Ring with a description of your floors and Zlata will give you a straight quote.

What is included

  • Pre-polish clean and preparation of the floor surface
  • Timber and hardwood floor polishing
  • Tile and stone buffering and polish
  • Vinyl and laminate floor restoration
  • Scuff mark and surface scratch treatment
  • Final buff to high finish

Often combined with

Floor polishing works best after an after-builders clean or a deep clean. If you are moving into a property or handing one over, Zlata can combine the full clean and floor polish into one visit. Mention it when you ring.

Floor type, condition and size all affect the price. Ring Zlata with the details.
