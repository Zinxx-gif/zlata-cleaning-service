Graiguenamanagh homes done right

Graiguenamanagh sits in a valley on the River Barrow, about 20km southeast of Kilkenny city near the Carlow border. The town is known for Duiske Abbey, one of the best-preserved Cistercian abbeys in Ireland, and for its setting between Brandon Hill and the river. It is a distinctive place, and the homes here reflect that character.

Zlata covers Graiguenamanagh as part of her south Kilkenny run. The drive is worth it. She brings 15 years of professional cleaning experience and a decade of running her own business, and she applies exactly the same standard here as anywhere else on her patch.

All quotes are per job. She doesn't charge by the hour. She assesses the property, gives you a fair price, and delivers. Ring or WhatsApp with a description of what you need.

Coverage includes Graiguenamanagh, Tinnahinch and the surrounding townlands along the Barrow valley.
