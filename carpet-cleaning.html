🧹 Deep clean for carpets

Carpet Cleaning Service Kilkenny

Carpets hold onto more than just dust. Zlata gives them a proper deep clean that a standard hoover doesn't touch.

Carpets that feel clean again

Carpets in a busy home collect more than surface dust. Pet hair, fine debris, spills that were wiped but not gone, odours that build up over time. A standard vacuum keeps the surface presentable, but it doesn't clean the carpet. That takes a proper deep clean.

Zlata has 15 years of experience and knows how to treat different carpet types without damaging them. She works through the carpet methodically, treating stains and built-up areas, and finishes with a thorough vacuum to lift everything out. The result is a carpet that feels different underfoot, not just one that looks a bit better.

All carpet cleaning is quoted per room or per property. Ring or WhatsApp, describe the rooms involved and the condition of the carpets, and Zlata will come back with a clear price.

What is included

  • Pre-treatment of visible stains and high-traffic areas
  • Deep vacuum on all carpeted areas
  • Spot treatment for stubborn marks
  • Edge and corner cleaning
  • All carpet types treated appropriately
  • Rugs included on request

Often booked alongside

Carpet cleaning is often paired with a deep clean of the full property, or as part of an end of tenancy clean. Mention what else needs doing when you ring and Zlata will factor it into one combined quote.
