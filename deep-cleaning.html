Deep Cleaning Service Kilkenny

The full reset. Top to bottom, nothing missed. For when a standard clean is not enough.

The full top-to-bottom

A deep clean is exactly what it sounds like. Every surface, every corner, every hidden spot that gets ignored during a standard clean. Inside the oven, inside cupboards, behind appliances, under furniture. Skirting boards, light switches, door handles, lime scale on taps. The works.

It takes 4 to 5 hours as a minimum, longer on larger properties. Zlata works through the house methodically. Nothing is rushed. She doesn't stop until it is done properly.

A deep clean is ideal as a one-off reset, before an event, after a period when the house has been neglected, or as the starting point before moving onto regular maintenance cleans.

What is included

  • Everything in the standard clean
  • Oven cleaned inside and out
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Inside all cupboards and drawers
  • Behind and under appliances
  • Under furniture where accessible
  • Window sills and frames (inside)
  • Skirting boards throughout
  • Light switches and door handles wiped
  • Lime scale removed from bathroom taps, shower and tiles
  • Grout scrubbed
  • Extractor fans dusted

When to book a deep clean

End-of-tenancy handover. Moving into a new home. Before hosting family. After a period of illness. Spring clean. After a renovation. Any of these are good reasons. Ring Zlata and she will advise whether a deep clean or an after-builders clean is the right fit for your situation.
